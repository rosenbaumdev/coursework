**Theme:** This course doesn't exist yet — on purpose.

**The pitch:**
> "Most courses are built for a generic student and you get slotted in. This one is built backwards: you talk first, and the next six weeks get shaped around what you actually know, what you're into, and what you want to build. Nothing here is real until you've done the interview."

### Start Here
Go to **[the onboarding interview](/zachary/interview)** and talk it through — what you've made, how you see the world right now, and what you actually care about. It takes about an hour. There are no wrong answers and it isn't a test.

When you're done, your real Day 1 gets written from what you said.
