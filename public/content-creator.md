description: Lock in your specific niche, define your brand voice, and write a one-page brand bible.

week: 1

---

Set up Node.js, Claude Code, and the Anthropic API in the project folder.
Build hook-generator.js to produce 5 viral opening lines from any topic, then script-writer.js for 45-75 second video scripts using the chosen hook.

By session end you can take a topic from idea to ready-to-record in under 10 minutes.
