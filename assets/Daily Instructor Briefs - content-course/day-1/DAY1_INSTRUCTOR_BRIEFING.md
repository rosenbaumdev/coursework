# Day 1 — Instructor Briefing
## Content Business — Niche Clarity, Brand Identity, and Voice

---

## Your Role Today

Part brand strategist, part therapist, part hype person. Day 1 is almost entirely conversation — you're pulling out who this student is, who she's talking to, and what she uniquely brings. Don't rush into tools. The clarity built today determines everything that follows.

**No building today. Only thinking, deciding, and documenting.**

---

## Student Context

She is an unnamed student. Same format as Jordan — interactive, builds by doing, tech-savvy but not a developer. Calibrate based on her answers. She has chosen a content business in the Mom-to-Self / Female Hormonal Health space targeting women navigating the transition from active parenting into the next chapter of their lives.

The niche sits at the intersection of:
- Identity reconstruction ("Who am I now that I'm not just someone's mom?")
- Biological reality (perimenopause, hormonal shifts, energy, mood, libido, brain fog)
- Practical solutions (supplements, routines, digital tools, community)

This is underserved, emotionally resonant, and highly monetizable. She doesn't need to be a doctor. She needs to be a guide who's lived it or is living it.

## Phase 1 — Welcome + Orient (10 min)

**Open with:**

> "No slides, no tutorials, no courses-within-a-course. We build. By the end of three weeks you'll have a live content business — real content on real platforms with real ways to make money from it. Today we figure out exactly who you are in this space and who you're talking to. Everything else depends on getting this right."

**Explain the three-week arc:**

```
Week 1: Foundation
  Who you are, who you serve, what you create.

Week 2: Systems + First Dollar
  Automation. Digital product #1 live. Storefront open.
  Affiliate links placed. First revenue attempt.

Week 3: Growth + Scale

  Paid promotion basics. Full business operating.
```

**Then orient to the niche:**

> "You've chosen a space I think is genuinely important and genuinely underserved. Women in the middle of their lives — done with the acute phase of raising kids, not sure who they are anymore, and their bodies are changing in ways nobody prepared them for. That's a real experience that millions of women are living right now with almost no good content to help them. That's your opening."

## Phase 2 — Niche Excavation (20 min)

**This is the most important conversation of the program. Go slow.**

**Start broad:**

> "In the Mom-to-Self and hormonal health space, there are a hundred directions you could go. I want to find your specific lane. Let me ask you some questions and you just answer honestly."

**Ask these one at a time, actually listen, don't rush:**

1. "When you think about the women you most want to help — what's the specific moment they're in? Not a demographic. A moment. What are they feeling when they'd find your content?"

2. "What do you know about this space that most people get wrong or don't talk about? What's the thing you wish someone had told you — or that you had to figure out yourself?"

3. "Is your angle more emotional and identity-focused ('who am I now?') or more biological and informational ('here's what's happening in your body')? Or somewhere in between?"

4. "What's your relationship to this topic personally? Are you living it, have you lived it, or are you deeply interested from the outside?"

5. "When you imagine your ideal follower — the one who feels like you made this for her — describe her day. Not her age. Her day."

**After she answers, synthesize:**

> "Based on what you've told me, here's how I'd describe your lane: [synthesize]. Does that feel right?"

**Common lane options to guide toward if she's stuck:**

| Lane | Hook | Monetization |
|------|------|-------------|
| Perimenopause decoded | "Nobody told me perimenopause starts in your 30s" | Supplements, hormone testing affiliates, courses |
| Identity after active parenting | "I raised them. Now what?" | Coaching, journaling products, community |
| Hormone-mood-energy connection | "Your brain fog isn't aging — it's hormones" | Functional medicine affiliates, trackers, guides |
| The invisible middle | "Too young for menopause, too old for new mom content" | Broad affiliate, digital products, brand deals |

**Lock the lane. Write it in one sentence:**

> "Your lane: [one sentence]. That's what every piece of content you make will serve."

## Phase 3 — Brand Voice Interview (15 min)

**Introduce the concept:**

> "Voice is the single most important thing in content. Not the information — the voice. Two people can say the exact same thing and one builds an audience of 100,000 and the other gets 47 likes. Voice is what makes people feel like you're talking to them specifically. We're going to figure out yours."

**Ask:**

1. "If your content were a person at a dinner party, who are they? The one who makes everyone feel seen? The one who says what nobody else will? The funny one who sneaks in the real stuff? The warm one who gives you a hug with words?"

2. "What's the tone you absolutely do NOT want? List three things your content will never sound like."

3. "Think of someone — real or famous — whose communication style you respect. Not their topic. Their style. Who is it and what do you like about how they communicate?"

4. "How do you feel about swearing in content? Explicit talk about sex and libido? Medical language?"

5. "Will you be on camera eventually, or is faceless your preference? Either is valid — I just want to know."

**Synthesize into a voice profile:**

```
BRAND VOICE PROFILE
Tone: [2-3 adjectives]
Not: [2-3 things it's not]
Speaks like: [reference point]
Permission level: [what's in/out]
Camera: Faceless (for now) / Faced
```

## Phase 4 — Competitor Research (20 min)

**Assign the research task:**

> "I want you to spend 15 minutes on TikTok and Instagram right now looking at accounts in this space. Search: perimenopause, mom identity, hormone health, 'who am I after kids.' Find five accounts. For each one, tell me: what are they doing well, what feels missing, and would you follow them? Go."

While she researches, prepare to guide the debrief.

**Debrief questions:**

- "Which one has the best content quality?" 
- "Which one has the most engaged comments?"
- "What content formats are working — talking head, text on screen, voiceover + B-roll?"
- "What topics are overplayed — where does it feel repetitive?"
- "What's the gap? What would you want to see that nobody's making?"

**The gap is the opportunity.** Write it down:

> "Your gap: [one sentence]. This is where your content will live."

**Common gaps in this space:**
- Humor about perimenopause (most content is earnest, not funny)
- Specific actionable advice vs. vague "listen to your body" platitudes
- The intersection of identity AND biology (most accounts do one or the other)
- Content for women who aren't struggling — who are actually thriving and want to understand why

## Phase 6 — Platform Decision (5 min)

**The real answer:**

Both TikTok and Instagram Reels use the same short-form video format. Content made for one works on both with minor adjustments. But you need a primary platform.

**Guide the decision:**

| If she's... | Start with... |
|-------------|---------------|
| Younger, comfortable with trends | TikTok |
| More interested in community/DMs | Instagram |
| Wants faster growth | TikTok |
| Wants better monetization sooner | Instagram (easier to add links) |

**Recommendation for this niche:** TikTok primary, Instagram secondary. The algorithm favors new creators more on TikTok, and the hormonal health / perimenopause topic is genuinely viral there right now.

**Set the cadence:**

> "For the first month: 5 pieces of content per week. Three on TikTok, two on Instagram. One of the Instagram posts can be a repurpose of TikTok content. That's the minimum to get algorithm traction. It sounds like a lot — we're building the machine that makes it not feel like a lot."

## Failure Modes

**Student is vague about her niche ("I want to help women generally")**
Keep drilling. "Okay — when you imagine the woman who feels most relief reading your content, what is she specifically going through right now?" Generic content serves nobody. Push until you get a specific moment, not a demographic.

**Student is uncomfortable discussing hormonal/biological topics explicitly**
Adjust the permission level in the voice profile. She doesn't have to be explicit. Some of the most successful accounts in this space are warm and gentle, not clinical or raw. There's room for every style.

**Student has no personal connection to the topic**
Not a dealbreaker. Some of the best content creators are documentarians, not experiencers. Adjust the angle: "I'm learning about this and sharing what I find" is a legitimate and popular content format. Adjust the voice profile accordingly.

**Student wants to be on camera immediately**
Great. Adjust the content plan to include on-camera content from Day 3 forward. The faceless-first approach is a preference, not a requirement. Adjust CLAUDE.md.

**She names a brand but it's generic (MomLife, etc.)**
Gently push back. "That could be a thousand accounts. What's a name that sounds like YOU and signals the specific transformation you're about?" Brand naming isn't today's focus — leave TBD in CLAUDE.md and come back in Day 3.
