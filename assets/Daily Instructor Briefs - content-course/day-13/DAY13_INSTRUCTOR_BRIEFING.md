## Phase 1 — Paid Promotion Philosophy (10 min)

**The rule:**

> "Paid promotion amplifies what's already working. It does NOT fix content that isn't working. If a video is getting 3% watch time organically, boosting it will get you expensive 3% watch time. If a video is getting 70% watch time organically, boosting it will get you cheap, effective reach. Only promote your winners."

**When paid promotion makes sense:**

- You have a video with strong organic performance (good watch time, saves, shares)
- You have something to sell (a product, a lead magnet)
- You can afford to lose the money (treat it as a learning cost)
- You have a measurable goal (email signups, product sales, followers)

**The $20 test:**

> "We're not trying to scale today. We're running a scientific test: spend $10 on TikTok and $10 on Instagram on the same piece of content, measure the results, and decide if the economics make sense to scale. $20 is cheap tuition."

## Phase 2 — Which Content to Promote (10 min)

**Selection criteria:**

Look at all posted content. Find the video that has:
- Highest watch time % (most important)
- Most saves or shares
- Most profile visits
- A clear CTA to the lead magnet or product

If it's the lead magnet video (free resource), even better — email list growth is the promotion goal, not direct sales.

**The math:**

```
If promoting lead magnet:
Spend: $10 on TikTok
Target: 1,000 additional views
If 5% click link in bio: 50 clicks
If 20% of clickers sign up: 10 email subscribers
Cost per subscriber: $1.00

Then: welcome sequence → product #1 at $27
If 10% of subscribers buy: 1 sale = $27
ROI: $10 spend → $27 revenue = 170% ROI (good)
```

> "This math only works if every piece of it is set up correctly. That's why we built the pipeline first — the email sequence, the store, the product. The ad is just a faucet that sends people into a working system."

## Phase 3 — TikTok Spark Ads (15 min)

**TikTok's Spark Ads are the right format:**

Spark Ads promote an existing organic TikTok post — not a separate ad. The post keeps its likes and comments. This matters for credibility.

**Setup:**

1. TikTok Ads Manager → ads.tiktok.com → Sign up (business account)
2. Create Campaign → Traffic or Lead Generation
3. Budget: $10 lifetime
4. Audience targeting:
   - Gender: female
   - Age: 30-50
   - Interests: Health & Fitness, Self-improvement, Parenting
   - Location: US, Canada, Australia, UK (English-speaking markets with buying power)
5. Ad format: Spark Ad → select the organic post
6. CTA: "Learn More" → her Stan Store or landing page URL
7. Run for 3 days (let it optimize)

---

## Phase 4 — Instagram Boost (10 min)

**Simpler than TikTok Ads:**

1. Go to the post she wants to boost on Instagram
2. Tap "Boost Post"
3. Goal: "More profile visits" or "More website visits"
4. Audience: create custom (female, 30-50, interests in wellness, hormones, parenting)
5. Budget: $10 over 3 days
6. Link: her Stan Store URL
