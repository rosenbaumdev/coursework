## Phase 5 — Mindset for the Long Game (10 min)

**The honest conversation:**

> "Most people who start content businesses quit within 60 days. Not because they weren't talented or the niche wasn't right. Because the early numbers feel small and the effort feels large. The math of content compounding is invisible until month 3-4, then suddenly obvious. The creators who win are the ones who kept posting at 200 followers with the same energy they had at 20,000."

**The things that will happen that will feel like failure but aren't:**

- A video with 47 views after one with 4,700 → normal. Inconsistency is normal.
- A week with no sales → normal at this stage
- A collaboration that doesn't go anywhere → normal
- Losing 20 followers in one day → normal (someone posted something controversial and followers purged — ignore it)
- A month where it feels like nothing is working → normal. Keep going.

**The one non-negotiable:**

> "Post five times a week for 90 days. That's the only rule. Everything else is optional, adjustable, and improvable. The one thing that isn't optional is consistency. If you do that one thing, the business has a real chance. If you don't, the tools and systems we built don't matter."
