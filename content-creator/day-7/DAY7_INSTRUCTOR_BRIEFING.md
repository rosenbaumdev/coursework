## Phase 1 — Storefront Platform (10 min)

**The options:**

| Platform | Fee | Best for |
|----------|-----|---------|
| Stan Store | $29/mo (14-day free trial) | Creators — all-in-one link in bio + store |
| Gumroad | 10% per sale | Simple, no monthly fee |
| Payhip | 5% per sale (free) | Budget-friendly |
| Beacons | Free / $10/mo | Link in bio focus |

**Recommendation: Stan Store**

> "Stan Store is built specifically for creators. Your link in bio, your store, your digital products, your affiliate links, and eventually your email list — all in one place. The $29/month looks like a cost but it's actually a revenue tool. You'll make it back with one sale."

**But:** Start the 14-day free trial. If she makes sales in 14 days — she keeps it. If not — reassess.

**Gumroad is the free fallback.** If budget is tight, start on Gumroad (free, 10% per sale) and migrate to Stan Store when revenue justifies it.

**Set up Stan Store:**

1. stan.store → Get started
2. Connect TikTok and Instagram
3. Set up payment: Stripe (create free account) → connect bank
4. Upload profile photo (brand graphic from Canva)
5. Write bio: same as social bios

## Phase 2 — Product Listed (15 min)

**Upload to Stan Store:**

1. Add product → Digital download
2. Upload the PDF from Day 6
3. Product name: the best title from listing copy
4. Price: $[17-27]
5. Paste short description from listing-copy.md
6. Upload cover image: create a simple product mockup in Canva
   (Canva has "book mockup" templates — take 5 minutes, looks professional)
7. Publish

**Test purchase:**

Use a second email to buy the product herself with a test card. Verify:
- Payment goes through
- Download link works
- PDF looks right
- Confirmation email arrives

> "You just bought your own product. That's the full customer experience. Was it smooth? Anything confusing? Fix it now before real buyers see it."

## Phase 4 — Link-in-Bio Setup (10 min)

**Stan Store IS the link in bio. Set it up:**

Sections on the Stan Store page:
1. **Header**: brand name + tagline
2. **Featured**: the digital product (prominent)
3. **Recommended Products**: affiliate links (Amazon storefront or individual)
4. **Follow Me**: TikTok and Instagram links
5. **Free Resource**: (placeholder for the lead magnet we build Day 9)

**Update every platform:**

TikTok bio → link: stan.store/[handle]
Instagram bio → link: stan.store/[handle]

> "Every piece of content you make from this point forward has this link available. Every viewer can become a customer. The store is always open."
